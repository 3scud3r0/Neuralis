A traceable reinforcement-learning experiment

      
01
Observe
Repeat each action for four emulator frames, max-pool recent frames, downsample to 84 × 84 and stack four observations.
02
Estimate
The CNN predicts a categorical action distribution and state value. Generalized advantage estimation handles deaths and timeouts separately.
03
Optimize
PPO constrains policy updates with clipping, regularizes exploration with entropy and limits gradient magnitude.
04
Measure
Evaluate held-out seeds. Export true native returns, flag completion, confidence intervals and numbered PNG evidence.
